Setting up

First you send the offer.

Vertical, payout, what has sold before. If it doesn’t suit organic video, we’ll tell you on the call.

Then we write the angles.

Concepts, scripts and references built around your buyer, before any page posts about it.

Next the pages start posting.

Short videos go out on our pages. Links in bio, caption links and automated DMs send viewers to your offer.

You pay when it sells.

Commission on the revenue we bring in. No retainer and no ad budget.
